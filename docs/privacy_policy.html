Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Moderator short-video publishing tool ("the Service") handles data.

  • Data we access. Only the owner's own TikTok/YouTube account authorization (OAuth tokens), used solely to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • Data we store. Generated videos and metadata are stored in the project's private repository and workflow artifacts. OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub secrets.
  • Sharing. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, other than sending videos to TikTok and YouTube for publishing.
  • Retention and deletion. Workflow artifacts expire automatically. You can request deletion or revoke access at any time via your TikTok/YouTube account settings or by contacting us.
