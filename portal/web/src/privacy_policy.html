Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Mind Decoded ("we", "us") publishes daily articles and short videos about psychology and artificial intelligence on this website and on our YouTube and TikTok channels. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, as permitted by the "openid email profile" scopes. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Content you submit. Comments, reactions and messages sent through the contact form, together with the name and email you choose to provide.
  • Technical and usage data. Basic, aggregated analytics such as pages viewed, approximate device type and referrer, used to understand which articles are useful.
  • Cookies and local storage. A session cookie to keep you signed in and small browser-storage entries for preferences. Embedded YouTube players are loaded from youtube-nocookie.com to limit tracking.

2. How we use information

  • to operate the site, sign you in and show your comments and reactions;
  • to reply to messages you send us;
  • to keep the service secure and prevent spam or abuse;
  • to improve our content.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. YouTube API Services

Our tools use YouTube API Services to upload our own videos and to list public videos of our own channel. By using this site you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We only access the data of our own channel; we do not access, store or share the YouTube data of site visitors. You can revoke any access this site has to your Google account at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

4. TikTok

Our tools use the TikTok Content Posting API to publish our own videos to our own TikTok account. We do not collect or store personal data of TikTok users through this integration. TikTok processes data under its own Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that run the site (hosting, database, sign-in), and when required by law. These providers may only use the data to provide their service to us.

6. Retention

Account details and comments are kept until you ask us to delete them or you delete your account. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to answer them.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. Use the contact page and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

We use HTTPS and access controls to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced on the site.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact page. See also our Terms of Service.
