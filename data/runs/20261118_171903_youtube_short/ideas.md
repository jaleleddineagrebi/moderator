## 3. The Invisible Hand Glitch
The viewer is told to scratch their left arm while looking into a mirror that hides their right arm. This triggers a 'proprioception error' where the brain feels the sensation in the wrong place. The video challenges viewers to try it with a friend or a mirror to 'break' their brain.

Hook (en): Your brain is easily fooled.
Why now: Visual and sensory illusions that can be performed at home have a high 'Average Percentage Viewed' (APV) because viewers watch once to understand the instruction and a second time to perform it alongside the narrator.
