## 2. The Troxler Effect Visual Hack
A live optical illusion where the viewer stares at a cross on screen until the surrounding colors vanish. This demonstrates the 'Troxler Effect' where neurons ignore unchanging stimuli. High APV because the viewer must stare for 15+ seconds.

Hook (en): Watch these colors literally disappear.
Why now: Visual sensory adaptation tests have a massive 'stickiness' factor. Viewers stay until the end to see if it works, often rewatching (APV >100%) to ensure they didn't blink or miss the transition.
Keywords: optical illusion, brain glitch, Troxler effect, vision hack, mind tricks
