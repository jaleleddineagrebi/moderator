# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why You Like People Near You More
The video explains the Propinquity Effect, where physical distance is the biggest predictor of friendship, surprising viewers by showing their 'choices' are just geography.

Hook (en): Your best friend is just a coincidence.
Hook (fr): Ton meilleur ami est juste une coincidence.
Why now: Social dynamics and friendship hacks consistently go viral on TikTok and Shorts; the proximity effect is a counter-intuitive 'hard truth' that challenges personal agency.
Keywords: psychology facts, propinquity effect, human behavior, psychologie sociale, amitié
- Festinger, Schachter, and Back (1950) - Social Pressures in Informal Groups
- Verywell Mind - The Propinquity Effect

## 2. Why Your Brain Hates Unfinished Tasks
It reveals why you can't stop thinking about an email you didn't send, showing how to use the Zeigarnik Effect to beat procrastination by just starting for 2 minutes.

Hook (en): Your brain hates unfinished business.
Hook (fr): Ton cerveau déteste les tâches inachevées.
Why now: Productivity and 'brain hacks' are high-retention topics. Recent search trends show a spike in 'dopamine detox' and 'focus' queries in both English and French markets.
Keywords: productivity hacks, zeigarnik effect, stop procrastination, psychologie travail, cerveau
- Bluma Zeigarnik (1927) - On Finished and Unfinished Tasks
- Psychology Today - The Zeigarnik Effect

## 3. Nobody is Actually Judging You
The video demonstrates the Spotlight Effect, proving people notice 50% less about your appearance or mistakes than you think, providing instant social anxiety relief.

Hook (en): Stop worrying, nobody is watching you.
Hook (fr): Arrête de t'inquiéter, personne ne te regarde.
Why now: Mental health and self-improvement content is evergreen. This specific bias performs well because it offers a 'relief' payoff, encouraging viewers to share it with anxious friends.
Keywords: social anxiety, spotlight effect, confidence tips, confiance en soi, psychologie
- Gilovich, Medvec, and Savitsky (2000) - The Spotlight Effect in Social Judgment
- American Psychological Association - Social Biases
