## 1. The Ghost Color Glitch
An interactive optical illusion where viewers stare at a specific dot to see a 'ghost' color on a gray screen. This forces 100% focus and prompts comments when the effect works.

Hook (en): Stare at this red dot.
Why now: Interactive illusions have the highest VVSA because they demand immediate participation. Similar 'color-filling' illusions are currently trending on TikTok and YouTube Shorts with millions of views due to the 'it actually worked' comment trigger.
Keywords: optical illusion, brain glitch, interactive test, afterimage effect, vision trick
- Opponent-Process Theory of Color Vision

## 3. The Thumb Lock Challenge
A physical 'brain-body' disconnect challenge where the viewer tries to move their fingers in a specific sequence that the brain struggle to coordinate. It forces rewatches to get it right.

Hook (en): You can't do this.
Why now: Negative hooks ('You can't...') have a 25% higher click-through rate. Physical challenges that look easy but are hard to execute drive massive 'I did it!' vs 'I failed' comment sections.
Keywords: brain challenge, body glitch, finger fitness, coordination test, brain hack
- Motor cortex inhibitory control studies
