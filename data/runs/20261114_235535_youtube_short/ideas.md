# Video ideas: Mind Hacks, AI Secrets, Bizarre Body Science & Hidden Power Dynamics

## 1. The IKEA Maze Trick
Explains the 'Bulla Bulla' and 'Fixed Path' layout that triggers impulsive buying. Challenges viewers to comment if they've ever 'only gone for lightbulbs' but left with a full cart. Built-in comment loop asking for their most useless purchase.

Hook (en): You’re being legally kidnapped by furniture stores.
Why now: Retail psychology content is currently peaking due to rising inflation sensitivity. The 'IKEA effect' and layout manipulation are viral staples that trigger high relatability and 'storytime' comments.
Keywords: ikea effect, consumer psychology, money traps, bulla bulla, shopping hacks
- The Gruen Effect / IKEA Way Research
- University College London (UCL) Architecture Study on IKEA Layouts

## 2. The Popcorn Price Trap
An interactive choice test where you show three prices (Small $3, Medium $6.50, Large $7). Explains how the 'Decoy' (Medium) forces your brain to pick the Large. Forces viewers to pause and choose, then comment their choice.

Hook (en): Stop picking the middle price option.
Why now: Interactive 'choose one' videos have 20% higher retention. Decoy pricing is a classic behavioral economics trick that feels like a 'secret' when exposed.
Keywords: decoy effect, pricing strategy, money secrets, billionaire tactics, asymmetric dominance
- Dan Ariely - Predictably Irrational
- The Economist subscription experiment (MIT study)

## 3. The $20 Auction Trap
The viewer is told they are in an auction for a $20 bill, but the second-place bidder also has to pay. Explains why smart people end up paying $50 for a $20 bill due to 'Loss Aversion'. Command to comment 'I'd quit' if they think they're smarter than the trap.

Hook (en): You’d pay fifty dollars for this twenty.
Why now: Game theory puzzles are exploding on TikTok/Shorts because they challenge the viewer's ego. The 'Dollar Auction' is a famous paradox that guarantees high debate in the comments.
Keywords: sunk cost fallacy, loss aversion, game theory, money traps, psychological warfare
- Martin Shubik's Dollar Auction Game Theory
- Harvard Business Review on Escalation of Commitment
