# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Your Brain Can't Forget Unfinished Tasks
The video explains the Zeigarnik Effect, where the brain prioritizes incomplete tasks, and reveals a simple 'shutdown' trick to stop overthinking at night.

Hook (en): You can't stop thinking about it.
Hook (fr): Tu n'arrives pas à arrêter d'y penser.
Why now: Brain-hack content is currently peaking on Shorts with users seeking relief from burnout and mental clutter. The Zeigarnik Effect provides a scientific explanation for a universal frustration.
Keywords: psychology, brain hacks, productivity, overthinking, Zeigarnik effect, psychologie, astuces cerveau, productivité, rumination
- Zeigarnik, B. (1927). Über das Behalten von erledigten und unerledigten Handlungen.
- American Psychological Association - The Zeigarnik Effect

## 2. Why Being Clumsy Makes You More Likable
It reveals the 'Pratfall Effect,' showing that highly competent people become more attractive and relatable when they make a small mistake, challenging the pressure to be perfect.

Hook (en): Stop trying to be perfect.
Hook (fr): Arrête d'essayer d'être parfait.
Why now: Social anxiety and 'perfectionism' are trending topics among Gen Z and Millennials. This counter-intuitive advice provides instant relief and a shareable 'aha' moment.
Keywords: social dynamics, attraction, charisma, Pratfall effect, likability, psychologie sociale, charisme, attraction, vulnérabilité
- Aronson, E., Willerman, B., & Floyd, J. (1966). The effect of a pratfall on increasing interpersonal attractiveness.
- Social Psychology Quarterly

## 3. Why Having Too Many Options Is Ruining Your Life
Based on the 'Jam Study,' the video shows how choice overload leads to decision paralysis and less satisfaction, explaining why we struggle with Netflix or menus.

Hook (en): Too many choices are making you miserable.
Hook (fr): Trop de choix te rend malheureux.
Why now: Decision fatigue is a major talking point in modern lifestyle minimalism. Relatable examples like 'choosing a movie' create high retention and comment engagement.
Keywords: decision fatigue, paradox of choice, happiness, brain science, shopping habits, fatigue décisionnelle, par paradoxe du choix, bonheur, neuroscience
- Iyengar, S. S., & Lepper, M. R. (2000). When choice is demotivating.
- The Paradox of Choice - Barry Schwartz
