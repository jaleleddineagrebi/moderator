# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Nobody Noticed Your Mistake
The video reveals that others notice our flaws 50% less than we think due to the Spotlight Effect. It uses generic city footage of crowds to show how everyone is focused on themselves, not you.

Hook (en): Nobody is actually looking at you.
Why now: Search interest in social anxiety and 'main character syndrome' is peaking. This provides a psychological 'relief' that encourages saves and repeats.
Keywords: psychology facts, social anxiety, spotlight effect, human behavior, self consciousness
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (Gilovich, Medvec, and Savitsky, 2000)
- American Psychological Association

## 2. Why Your Brain Won't Shut Up
It explains the Zeigarnik Effect—why unfinished tasks haunt your thoughts while finished ones are forgotten. It shows how 'open loops' create mental tension that keeps you awake.

Hook (en): Your brain hates unfinished business.
Why now: The 'productivity' and 'sleep' niches are massive. Explaining the 'why' behind a common frustration like 'racing thoughts' drives high engagement.
Keywords: productivity tips, brain hacks, zeigarnik effect, procrastination, mental health
- Bluma Zeigarnik (1927) Research on Task Interruption
- British Psychological Society

## 3. The Secret to Making People Like You
This flips social logic by explaining that people like you more after they do YOU a favor. It uses the Ben Franklin Effect to show how the brain justifies its actions through cognitive dissonance.

Hook (en): Want someone to like you? Ask for a favor.
Why now: Counter-intuitive social 'hacks' perform exceptionally well in the 'dark psychology' and self-improvement sub-niches on YouTube Shorts.
Keywords: social skills, dark psychology, ben franklin effect, persuasion, friendship tips
- Jecker and Landy (1969) study on liking
- The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin
