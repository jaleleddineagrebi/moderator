## 1. The Ghost Green Illusion
An interactive optical test where the viewer stares at a specific point to force their brain to generate a color that isn't on the screen. It triggers massive comments because viewers want to report if it worked for them.

Hook (en): Stare at the black dot.
Why now: Interactive illusions have extremely high VVSA because they give a direct command. The Troxler Effect and negative afterimages consistently go viral as they provide a 'physical' sensation of the brain glitching.
Keywords: optical illusion, brain test, interactive challenge, vision hack, psychology facts
- Troxler's Fading Effect

Hook (en): Stop watching their eyes.
Why now: Social survival and 'reading minds' are high-velocity topics. This specific micro-behavior is less known than eye contact, making it feel like a 'forbidden' secret.

## 3. The Finger Velocity Glitch
An interactive physical challenge where viewers waggle their fingers in front of a TV or LED light to see a 'stroboscopic' effect. It forces a 100% completion rate as they try it while watching.

Hook (en): You can't see reality.
Why now: Interactive physical hacks have the highest retention (APV) because the viewer is physically engaged with the screen. This exploit of the 'Persistence of Vision' is a reliable viral trigger.
