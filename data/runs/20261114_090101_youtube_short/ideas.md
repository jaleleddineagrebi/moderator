Hook (en): Don't blink or you'll miss this.
Why now: Interactive illusions have some of the highest APV (Average Percentage Viewed) on the platform because they require the viewer to stay until the very last frame for the 'payoff'.
Keywords: brain glitch, optical illusion, interactive test, vision hack, brain games
