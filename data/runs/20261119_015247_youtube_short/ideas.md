## 2. The Negative Afterimage Brain Glitch
The video shows a high-contrast inverted image and forces the viewer to stare at a white dot for 15 seconds. When the screen turns blank white, they see a full-color image that isn't there. High retention due to the required 'staring' time.

Hook (en): Stare at this dot. Do not blink.
Why now: Forced-focus challenges (Type 1 Archetype) guarantee high VVSA because they present an immediate physical challenge. The physiological 'payoff' at the end ensures the viewer watches until the final second.
Keywords: optical illusion, brain glitch, vision hack, interactive test, human eye secrets
- Opponent Process Theory of Color Vision (Hering)
- Negative Afterimage Neural Adaptation
