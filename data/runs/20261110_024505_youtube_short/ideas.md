# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why You Freeze Before Starting Work
Explains the psychological phenomenon of task paralysis and the amygdala hijacking your prefrontal cortex when an ambition feels ambiguous, revealing the counter-intuitive 2-minute fix that tricks the brain into action.

Hook (en): You are not actually lazy.
Why now: Executive dysfunction and task freezing are currently experiencing massive viral engagement across TikTok and YouTube Shorts under burnout and ADHD-adjacent discourse, consistently earning high retention due to immediate self-identification.
Keywords: procrastination psychology, task paralysis, executive dysfunction, brain hacks, productivity tips
- Ferrari, J. R. (2010). Still Procrastinating: The No-Regrets Guide to Getting It Done
- Pychyl, T. A. (2013). Solving the Procrastination Puzzle

## 2. Why Small Talk Drains Your Battery
Reveals that small talk triggers cognitive friction because your brain expends energy calculating social masks and shallow scripts rather than engaging the default mode network involved in genuine human connection.

Hook (en): Why does small talk feel exhausting?
Why now: Social fatigue and the 'social battery' concept dominate evergreen short-form psychology feeds, particularly among Gen Z and millennial audiences looking for clinical validation of everyday social friction.
Keywords: introvert psychology, social battery, small talk anxiety, social dynamics, human connection
- Mehl, M. R., et al. (2010). Eavesdropping on Happiness: Well-being is Related to Having Less Small Talk and More Substantive Conversations. Psychological Science
- Kahneman, D. (2011). Thinking, Fast and Slow

## 3. Why You Suddenly Wake Up at 3 AM
Demystifies the universal midnight panic wake-up by showing how the 3 AM cortisol surge pairs with low blood sugar to make small worries feel like life-or-death emergencies.

Hook (en): Ever wake up panicking at three?
Why now: Midnight awakening and catastrophizing affect millions daily; explaining the physiological mechanism behind sudden existential dread provides immediate relief and triggers strong share-to-friends loops.
Keywords: waking up at 3am, cortisol awakening, sleep anxiety, circadian rhythm, brain chemistry
- Walker, M. (2017). Why We Sleep: Unlocking the Power of Sleep and Dreams
- Russell, G., & Lightman, S. (2019). The human circulating ultradian rhythm of cortisol. Frontiers in Neuroendocrinology
