# Video ideas: Mind Hacks, AI Secrets, Bizarre Body Science & Hidden Power Dynamics

## 1. The Coffee Price Trap
The viewer is presented with 3 prices and forced to pick the 'best deal' in 3 seconds; this reveals the Decoy Effect where the middle option exists only to make the most expensive one look like a bargain. Comment trigger: Ask viewers if they fell for the 'Medium' trap.

Hook (en): Pick the best deal fast.
Why now: Interactive 'choice' videos have the highest retention because they force the viewer to engage mentally before the reveal. The Decoy Effect is a proven viral psychological concept used by Starbucks and Apple.
Keywords: decoy effect, consumer psychology, money traps, pricing secrets, marketing tricks
- Dan Ariely - Predictably Irrational (Decoy Effect research)
- Journal of Consumer Research - Asymmetric Dominance Effect

## 2. The 3-Word Negotiator
Shows a social power hack where repeating the last 3 words of a seller's sentence forces them to lower prices or reveal secrets through the 'Mirroring' effect. Forces viewers to visualize their last negotiation. Subscribe trigger: 'Use this to save thousands.'

Hook (en): Repeat their last three words.
Why now: Social power moves and 'FBI level' negotiation tactics are currently trending on high-performance finance shorts. It provides immediate, actionable value which drives high 'Save' and 'Share' counts.
Keywords: negotiation hacks, social power, dark psychology, Chris Voss, money saving tips
- Chris Voss - Never Split the Difference (Mirroring Technique)
- Behavioral Science - Isopraxism study

## 3. The Red Tag Scam
A visual demonstration showing how your brain bypasses logic when it sees the color red on a price tag, even if there is no actual discount. It triggers the 'Survival Brain' to buy before investigating. Comment trigger: 'Have you ever bought something just because it was red?'

Hook (en): Red prices are lying.
Why now: This idea leverages a 'hidden truth' about everyday environments (supermarkets), creating a high 'Curiosity Gap.' It falls under the 'Insane Money Secrets' archetype which typically sees >100% APV due to the 'shock' reveal.
Keywords: retail secrets, shopping hacks, color psychology, impulse buying, money traps
- Psychology of Color in Marketing - University of Winnipeg
- Journal of Retailing - The effect of price-color congruency
