## 1. The 10-Second Vision Eraser
The viewer stares at a central cross on a blurry, multi-colored background. Because of the Troxler Effect, the brain stops processing static peripheral data, causing the colors to literally vanish from sight. This forces 100% focus and generates 'it worked' comments.

Hook (en): Stare at the center and watch everything vanish.
Why now: Optical illusions that happen 'live' in the video have the highest retention rates (105%+) because viewers must watch multiple times to ensure they didn't just blink or miss the change.
Keywords: vision glitch, brain test, optical illusion, Troxler effect, interactive test
- Troxler's Fading (1804)
- Journal of Vision: Neural adaptation and peripheral fading

## 2. The Instant Memory Hijack
The video rapidly lists 8 words related to sleep (bed, rest, tired, pillow) but never says the word 'sleep.' When challenged at the end, 80% of viewers will 'remember' the word sleep being there. This exposes the DRM Effect and creates massive debate in the comments.

Hook (en): I'm going to hack your memory.
Why now: Tests that 'prove' the viewer wrong are highly shareable and spark 'how did he do that' comments, which signals the algorithm for explosive reach.
Keywords: memory test, brain hack, psychology trick, DRM paradigm, false memory
- Deese-Roediger-McDermott (DRM) Paradigm (1995)
- Harvard Psychology: False Memory Studies

Why now: Social survival hacks and relationship 'cheats' are currently high-velocity topics on Shorts, specifically those involving eye contact and group dynamics.
