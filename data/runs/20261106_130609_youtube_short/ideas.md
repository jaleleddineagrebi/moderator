# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Everyone Is Not Staring At You
This video exposes the Spotlight Effect, revealing how your brain hallucinates that strangers are judging your every micro-movement. It surprises viewers by proving that people barely remember what you wore an hour ago.

Hook (en): Nobody is looking at you.
Why now: High search volume around social anxiety and self-consciousness combined with an immediate counter-intuitive pattern interrupt hook guarantees high 3-second retention.
Keywords: spotlight effect, social anxiety psychology, why people judge you, psychology facts
- Thomas Gilovich Spotlight Effect Studies
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 2. Why Future You Always Gets Screwed Over
This video breaks down Present Bias, showing why your brain views your future self like a completely different stranger. It explains why you endlessly procrastinate and sabotage tomorrow for a tiny dose of today's comfort.

Hook (en): Your brain thinks future you is a stranger.
Why now: Procrastination and financial regret are universal daily frustrations. Frame it as neurological betrayal rather than laziness to trigger massive shareability.
Keywords: present bias, why do I procrastinate, behavioral psychology, brain hacks
- Hal Hershfield fMRI Future Self Studies
- Behavioral Economics Research

## 3. Why Having Too Many Options Ruins Your Life
This video explores the Paradox of Choice, demonstrating how adding just a few more options to a decision actually increases anxiety and stops people from pulling the trigger.

Hook (en): More choices make you miserable.
Why now: Everyone experiences choice fatigue daily from streaming platforms to supermarket aisles, making the insight immediately relevant and viral.
Keywords: paradox of choice, decision making psychology, overthinking habit, mental traps
- Barry Schwartz The Paradox of Choice
- Columbia University Jam Experiment
