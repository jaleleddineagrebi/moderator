# Video ideas: Mind Hacks, AI Secrets, Bizarre Body Science & Hidden Power Dynamics

## 1. The 3-Price Trap Making You Overspend
Exposes the classic asymmetric dominance effect (Decoy Effect) used by streaming services and tech giants, challenging viewers with a live choice between three pricing tiers to prove their brain was manipulated. Drives comments as viewers debate whether they fell for option B or C.

Hook (en): Stop picking the middle option right now.
Why now: Directly taps Archetype 1 & 2 by giving the viewer a live decision test on screen. The Decoy Effect is proven behavioral economics (MIT / Dan Ariely research) that triggers massive replay rates to verify the trick.
Keywords: decoy effect psychology, consumer pricing tricks, hidden money traps, dark psychology marketing
- MIT / Dan Ariely - Predictably Irrational (The Economist Pricing Experiment)
- Journal of Consumer Research - Asymmetric Dominance in Choice Architecture

## 2. Why You Love Discounts But Hate Surcharges
Reveals the Framing Effect and loss aversion trick where stores charge you identical amounts but frame one as a 'cash discount' instead of a 'card penalty' to bypass your brain's pain receptors. Asks viewers to comment whether they check itemized receipts.

Hook (en): Your brain hates penalties, not paying more.
Why now: Uses Archetype 2 (Forbidden Money Secrets) with immediate, visceral relevance to everyday checkout habits. Loss aversion creates high engagement because viewers realize their subconscious pain receptors are being hacked.
Keywords: framing effect psychology, loss aversion money trap, consumer mind tricks, behavioral economics hacks
- Daniel Kahneman & Amos Tversky - Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk
- Stanford Graduate School of Business - The Psychology of Surcharges vs Discounts

Why now: Delivers Archetype 3 (Social Power & Micro-Behavior Decoding). Meta-analyses of 42 field studies confirm the BYAF technique consistently doubles persuasion rates, delivering instant tactical value that viewers save and share.
