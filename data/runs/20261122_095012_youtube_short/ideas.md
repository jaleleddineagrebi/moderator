# Video ideas: Mind Hacks, AI Secrets, Bizarre Body Science & Hidden Power Dynamics

## 1. The McGurk Audio Glitch
Demonstrates the McGurk Effect where your eyes trick your ears into hearing different sounds. Viewer hears 'Ba' with eyes closed and 'Fa' with eyes open. Forces a rewatch to confirm the glitch and includes a call to subscribe and comment which word they heard first.

Hook (en): Close your eyes to hear it change.
Why now: This is a classic high-retention interactive trick. Because the effect is so jarring, viewers almost always rewatch the video to toggle between eyes open and eyes closed, guaranteeing an APV well over 100%.
Keywords: mcgurk effect, brain glitch, audio illusion, psychology, interactive test
- McGurk, H. and MacDonald, J. (1976). Hearing lips and seeing voices. Nature.
- Psychological Science (2014): The McGurk Effect and multisensory integration.

## 2. The Finger Magnet Brain Glitch
Uses the ideomotor effect to make the viewer's index fingers move toward each other without conscious effort. It feels like an invisible magnetic pull. Includes a call to subscribe for more hidden reflexes and comment 'TOUCHED' if their fingers met.

Hook (en): Your fingers are about to move themselves.
Why now: Physical 'spooky' reactions perform exceptionally well in the Short-form format because they provide immediate proof of the video's claim. This interactive test triggers high comment volume from people confirming it worked.
Keywords: ideomotor effect, finger magnet, brain hack, interactive challenge, human reflex
- Carpenter, W.B. (1852). On the influence of Suggestion in modifying and directing Muscular Movement.
- American Journal of Psychology: Studies on the Ideomotor Phenomenon.

## 3. The Painless Elbow Skin Glitch
Challenges the viewer to pinch the skin on their elbow (the weebis) as hard as possible to prove it has almost no pain receptors. It's a bizarre biological blind spot. Encourages viewers to subscribe for more body secrets and rate their pain from 1 to 10 in the comments.

Hook (en): Pinch your elbow skin right now.
Why now: This is a high-velocity 'challenge' hook that uses a specific command. It exploits curiosity about physical limits and is safe but surprising, leading to high VVSA as people stop to try it immediately.
Keywords: body glitch, elbow pinch, anatomy facts, bizarre science, human body secret
- Journal of Investigative Dermatology: Cutaneous innervation and receptor density.
- Gray's Anatomy: Sensory nerve distribution of the upper limb.
