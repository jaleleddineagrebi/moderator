# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Your Brain Can't Let Go of Unfinished Tasks
The video explains the Zeigarnik Effect, where your brain creates a state of mental tension that only resolves when a task is completed, which is why your to-do list feels physically heavy.

Hook (en): Your brain hates unfinished business.
Hook (fr): Ton cerveau déteste les tâches inachevées.
Why now: Productivity and 'brain fog' topics are currently surging on social media as users seek cognitive reasons for burnout. The Zeigarnik Effect is a proven psychological pillar for engagement.
Keywords: productivity, psychology, procrastination, brain hacks, productivité, psychologie, cerveau
- British Psychological Society - The Zeigarnik Effect
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology

## 2. The Secret Trick Behind Every Menu
The video reveals the 'Decoy Effect' where companies add a third, expensive option just to make the middle option look like a bargain, forcing your brain into a predictable choice.

Hook (en): You aren't actually choosing freely.
Hook (fr): Tu ne choisis pas vraiment librement.
Why now: Consumer behavior videos have high retention rates because they reveal a 'secret' about everyday life. Financial psychology is a high-CPM niche with global appeal.
Keywords: marketing psychology, manipulation, human behavior, money hacks, psychologie du consommateur, biais cognitif, argent
- Psychology Today - The Decoy Effect
- Journal of Consumer Research

## 3. Why Your Clumsiness Makes You Likable
The video illustrates the Pratfall Effect, showing that highly competent people who make a small mistake or trip are perceived as more attractive and relatable than those who are perfect.

Hook (en): Being perfect makes people dislike you.
Hook (fr): Être parfait te rend moins aimable.
Why now: Social dynamics and 'how to be likable' content performs exceptionally well in the Arabic and European markets where social hierarchy and etiquette are highly valued.
Keywords: social skills, attraction, body language, confidence, charisme, relations sociales, confiance en soi
- Social Psychology by Elliot Aronson
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology - The Pratfall Effect
