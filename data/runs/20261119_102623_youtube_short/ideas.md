# Video ideas: Mind Hacks, AI Secrets, Bizarre Body Science & Hidden Power Dynamics

## 1. The Red Hammer Brain Glitch
An interactive mental challenge where the narrator forces the viewer to think of a specific object and color through rapid-fire math. The reveal at the end causes massive comment engagement as viewers confirm if it worked or share their 'glitched' alternative.

Hook (en): I'm going to read your mind.
Why now: Interactive mind-reading tests have the highest VVSA because they require immediate participation. The 'Red Hammer' effect is a classic psychological priming trick that consistently goes viral by proving how predictable human heuristics are.
Keywords: mind reading test, brain glitch, psychology trick, interactive short

## 2. The Reverse Favor Power Move
A social power dynamic video explaining why asking for a small favor makes someone like you more than if you did a favor for them. It challenges the viewer to try it today and comment 'it worked' once they see the shift in respect.

Hook (en): Stop doing favors for people.
Why now: Counter-intuitive social advice triggers curiosity. The Ben Franklin Effect is a well-documented cognitive dissonance phenomenon that provides high-value 'social survival' knowledge, driving high APV and shares.
Keywords: dark psychology, social hacks, Ben Franklin effect, power dynamics
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
- Encyclopedia Britannica: Ben Franklin Effect

## 3. The Audio Illusion That Breaks Your Brain
A live interactive test using the McGurk Effect where the viewer hears a different sound depending on whether their eyes are open or closed. This forces the viewer to rewatch the video multiple times to 'test' their ears, skyrocketing the APV over 100%.

Hook (en): Close your eyes right now.
Why now: Sensory illusions are the 'gold standard' for 100%+ APV because viewers must loop the video to verify the effect. This specific glitch proves how your vision overrides your hearing, creating a high 'shock' factor.
Keywords: brain test, audio illusion, McGurk Effect, sensory glitch
- Nature: The McGurk Effect in Speech Perception
- BBC Science: How Your Eyes Can Fool Your Ears
