# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Favors Make Friends
It reveals the counter-intuitive truth that asking someone for a small favor actually makes them like you more, rather than doing a favor for them.

Hook (en): Ask for a favor to be liked.
Hook (fr): Demande un service pour être aimé.
Why now: Social hack videos are currently viral on TikTok and Shorts because they offer immediate 'cheat codes' for social life. The Ben Franklin effect is a classic curiosity gap topic.
Keywords: psychology hacks, social dynamics, Ben Franklin effect, human behavior, psychologie sociale, astuces sociales
- Jecker, J., & Landy, D. (1969). Liking a Person as a Function of Doing Him a Favour.

## 2. Why Your Flaws Are Attractive
It explains the Pratfall Effect: how highly competent people become significantly more likable and relatable when they make a small mistake or show a flaw.

Hook (en): Your mistakes make you more likable.
Hook (fr): Tes erreurs te rendent plus apprécié.
Why now: Relatable content regarding social anxiety and the pressure to be perfect performs well. This provides 'relief' to the viewer, encouraging shares and saves.
Keywords: attraction, likability, Pratfall effect, self improvement, charisme, erreurs
- Aronson, E., Willerman, B., & Floyd, J. (1966). The effect of a pratfall on increasing interpersonal attractiveness.

## 3. Why You Overvalue Your Work
It breaks down why we feel a stronger emotional bond and higher perceived value for things we had a hand in creating, even if they are objectively low quality.

Hook (en): You love it because you built it.
Hook (fr): Tu l'aimes car tu l'as construit.
Why now: The 'IKEA effect' is a trending search term in behavioral economics. It explains common household arguments and consumer habits, making it highly relatable for a general audience.
Keywords: IKEA effect, cognitive bias, consumer behavior, brain facts, biais cognitif, cerveau
- Norton, M. I., Mochon, D., & Ariely, D. (2012). The IKEA effect: When labor leads to love.
