# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why You Always Pick the Middle Option
This video exposes the Decoy Effect, showing how companies deliberately introduce a useless third option to trick your brain into buying the most expensive item. It surprises viewers by revealing that their purchasing choices are heavily manipulated by simple spatial framing.

Hook (en): You are being tricked into spending more.
Why now: Behavioral economics and pricing tricks are performing exceptionally well on Shorts right now because viewers instantly relate to making purchases and feeling manipulated.
Keywords: decoy effect, psychology of money, behavioral economics, marketing tricks
- Predictably Irrational by Dan Ariely
- Behavioral Economics studies on asymmetric dominance

## 2. Why Everyone Is Not Staring At You
This video explains the Spotlight Effect, proving that people remember your embarrassing moments a fraction as much as you think they do. It provides immediate relief and taps into universal social anxiety.

Hook (en): Nobody noticed your most embarrassing moment.
Why now: Self-improvement and social anxiety shorts consistently drive high retention and comment engagement as viewers reflect on their own insecurities.
Keywords: spotlight effect, social anxiety, psychology facts, overthinking
- Cornell University Spotlight Effect Studies by Thomas Gilovich

## 3. Make Someone Like You By Asking This
This video breaks down the Benjamin Franklin Effect, demonstrating the counterintuitive truth that asking someone for a small favor actually makes them like you more, rather than annoying them.

Hook (en): Want them to like you? Ask this.
Why now: Actionable social psychology hacks that alter interpersonal relationships have viral potential because viewers immediately want to test them in real life.
Keywords: ben franklin effect, psychology tricks, social dynamics, how to make friends
- Benjamin Franklin autobiography
- Studies on cognitive dissonance in social behavior
