# Video ideas: Psychology & human behavior (biases, habits, social dynamics)

## 1. Why Beginners Feel Like Experts
This video explains the Dunning-Kruger effect through the 'Peak of Mount Ignorant,' showing why people with the least knowledge often have the most confidence while experts doubt themselves.

Hook (en): Why are the least talented people so confident?
Hook (fr): Pourquoi les moins doués sont-ils si confiants ?
Why now: Educational 'aha' moments about social frustrations perform well; the Dunning-Kruger effect currently sees high search volume on TikTok and Reels as people seek to explain online behavior.
Keywords: Dunning-Kruger effect, psychology of confidence, cognitive bias, effet Dunning-Kruger, psychologie confiance en soi, biais cognitif
- Journal of Personality and Social Psychology (Kruger & Dunning, 1999)
- Psychology Today: The Dunning-Kruger Effect

## 2. Why Having Options Makes You Miserable
The Paradox of Choice reveals that more options lead to less satisfaction and higher anxiety. It explains why you can't pick a movie on Netflix or a meal at a restaurant.

Hook (en): Too many choices are ruining your happiness.
Hook (fr): Trop de choix gâchent ton bonheur.
Why now: Universal relatability regarding digital era 'overload.' Shorts focusing on daily frustrations like 'scrolling forever' see high retention rates due to instant recognition.
Keywords: Paradox of choice, decision fatigue, psychology of happiness, paradoxe du choix, fatigue décisionnelle, psychologie du bonheur
- Barry Schwartz: The Paradox of Choice
- American Psychological Association: Decision Fatigue

## 3. Your Hidden Sixth Sense
Most people think we only have five senses, but Proprioception—your brain's ability to know where your limbs are without looking—is what actually stops you from falling over.

Hook (en): You actually have a sixth sense.
Hook (fr): Tu as en réalité un sixième sens.
Why now: The 'fact-check' style hook (debunking the 5-senses myth) creates a curiosity gap. Biology-based psychology facts have a high 'shareability' factor on mobile platforms.
Keywords: Proprioception, human senses, brain facts, body awareness, proprioception, sens humains, faits sur le cerveau
- Harvard Medical School: The Science of Proprioception
- Nature Neuroscience: Body Awareness
